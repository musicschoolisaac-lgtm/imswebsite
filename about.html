About Harmony Music School Rwanda

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Harmony Music School was established to empower Rwandans through the universal language of music. Our mission is to provide high-quality, accessible music education that blends global standards with the rich rhythms and traditions of Rwanda.

We believe music has the power to transform lives, build confidence, and connect communities. Our dedicated team of professional musicians, educators, and performers brings years of experience from both local and international stages.

Our Vision

To be Rwanda’s leading center for musical excellence and creativity, inspiring a new generation of talented performers and educators.

Our Values

  • Creativity: Encouraging artistic expression and innovation.
  • Discipline: Building strong technical and performance skills.
  • Community: Promoting collaboration and cultural pride.
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in music education.

Community Partnerships

Harmony Music School continues to partner with schools, churches, and cultural organizations across Rwanda to promote music literacy and appreciation. Together, we are building a vibrant future for music in Rwanda — one note at a time.
