Welcome to Harmony Music School Rwanda

At Harmony Music School, we believe every student has a unique musical voice. Our expert instructors help learners of all ages explore, create, and perform music with confidence in modern facilities and a nurturing environment.

Our Mission

We aim to nurture talent and inspire creativity in every student. By combining traditional and contemporary teaching methods, we cultivate a passion for music and performance, preparing students to shine on both local and international stages.

Join our vibrant community of musicians and discover a world of musical possibilities.

Featured Programs

  • Piano & Keyboard Training – Beginner to Advanced
  • Guitar & Bass Lessons – Rock, Jazz, & Classical Styles
  • Voice & Vocal Coaching – Technique & Performance
  • Drums & Percussion – Rhythm Mastery
  • Traditional Rwandan Music & Dance – Cultural Enrichment
  • Music Theory & Composition – Unlock Your Creativity
